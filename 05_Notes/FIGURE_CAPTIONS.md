# Figure captions

**Figure 1.** Temporal harvest coverage of corn, soybean, and wheat across management systems, 1981–2024. Colors distinguish pre-2008 full-tillage observations, post-2008 paired FT/RT composites, and post-2008 single-arm harvests excluded from the primary composite series. Blank years represent crops not harvested in the system.

**Figure 2.** Model-standardized mean yield against detrended interannual SD with reference iso-CV lines. Pointwise 95% bootstrap intervals are shown when available. These are model-conditional estimates, not unadjusted year-to-year sample statistics.

**Figure 3.** Absolute CNV–organic yield gaps calculated only in shared crop-harvest years. Percent gaps are differences in matched-year means divided by matched-year CNV means. Points show observed annual gaps; dotted lines show pairwise mean differences.

**Figure 4.** Distribution of observed crop yield anomalies among harvest years shared by all three systems. The dashed line denotes an illustrative 20% below-mean threshold; samples are small, especially wheat.

**Figure 5.** Exploratory yield anomaly–SPI-3 associations with dry (SPI-3 <= -1) and wet (SPI-3 >= 1) regimes shaded. The plot is descriptive, not evidence of differential drought resistance.

**Figure 6.** RT minus FT yield differences in within-system paired crop-harvest years after 2008. Error bars are unadjusted paired-year 95% t intervals; conventional wheat is excluded from inferential comparisons due to n=2.

**Figure S1.** Matched-year difference-in-differences comparing organic versus CNV RT–FT effects when all four treatment arms were observed. Error bars are unadjusted paired-year 95% intervals. The graphic is exploratory.
